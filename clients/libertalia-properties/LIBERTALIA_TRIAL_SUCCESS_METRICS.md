# Libertalia Properties Trial Success Metrics

**Client:** Libertalia Properties
**Primary user:** Kopano Nkotsi
**Trial status:** 7-Day Free Trial
**North-star outcome:** Kopano receives a lead, AgentFlow qualifies it, AgentFlow assists with viewing booking, and Kopano sees immediate operational value.

## Trial Success Definition

The trial is successful if AgentFlow becomes useful to Kopano within the first 24–48 hours by reducing lead-handling friction.

Success does **not** depend on compliance automation, document verification, seller workflows, or any Phase 2 feature.

## First 24–48 Hour Activation Metrics

| Metric | Target | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client workspace ready | Yes | Libertalia profile, Kopano primary agent, branding placeholders, stages, and views configured. |
| First inbound lead captured | 1+ | Property24 or WhatsApp lead appears under Libertalia Properties. |
| Lead assigned to Kopano | 100% of trial leads | New leads default to Kopano. |
| Qualification context extracted | Basic context visible | Name/contact/source/property interest/message context captured where available. |
| Next useful question identified | Yes | AgentFlow shows or assists with the next qualification question when context is missing. |
| Viewing intent detected | When present | Lead moves to Viewing Requested or task is created. |
| Viewing booking assistance | 1 assisted flow | AgentFlow helps Kopano confirm/track a viewing step. |
| Task generated | Yes | Follow-up or viewing task exists for Kopano. |
| Workspace clarity | Yes | Lead appears in the correct guided view. |

## 7-Day Trial Metrics

## Qualitative Success Questions

Ask Kopano after the first real lead flow:

1. Did AgentFlow make it easier to see the lead and context?
2. Did AgentFlow help you know what to do next?
3. Did the workspace show the lead in the right place?
4. Did the viewing/follow-up task help you avoid forgetting the next action?
5. What felt confusing or unnecessary?

## Explicit Non-Metrics For This Trial

Do not judge this pilot on:

- Compliance workflow completeness
- Document collection or verification
- Rental application processing depth
- Offer to purchase processing
- Seller pipeline workflows
- LOOM integrations
- Fully automated sales operations
- Platform-wide architecture expansion

## Go / No-Go Signal

### Go signal

Proceed to deeper pilot hardening if:

- At least one real or controlled lead is captured.
- Kopano can qualify the lead using AgentFlow context.
- Kopano can move or act on a viewing-related next step.
- The workflow feels useful within 24–48 hours.

### No-go signal

Pause expansion if:

- Property24 or WhatsApp inputs are missing.
- Leads cannot be reliably routed to Libertalia.
- Kopano cannot see the lead/task clearly.
- The setup starts drifting into compliance, documents, seller workflows, or custom architecture.

## Phase 2 Parking Lot

Only after the trial proves immediate operational value:

- Deeper document handling
- Rental/application workflow refinement
- More granular pipeline analytics
- Additional users or branches
- Seller-side workflows
- Advanced automation

For now, the win is simple: **lead in, context clear, viewing next step assisted.**
